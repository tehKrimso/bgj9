### HeroesLastHope
Game made in 48 hours for BanzaiGameJam 9. Theme: Combinations.
Game design, programming and asset integratin done by one developer.

### About
You play as alchemist supporting tean of heroes in an auto-battler fight. Combine ingreidients in your flask to brew diffeerent potions wich help your allies to win the battle. The heroes fight on their own - your only way to help them is to decide what to brew and allie target on whom you use it.

### Gameplay
- Combine different ingredients, each ingredient have specific effect: heal, add damage, increase hero speed.
- Choose which hero to support at the right moment.
- Heroes battle automatically - timing and ingredients choise are everyting.

### Tech
- Engine: Unity
- Language: C#
